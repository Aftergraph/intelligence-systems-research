# Working Glossary

**Assurance** — umbrella for testing, evaluation, verification and validation.

**Authority** — permission for a principal to perform a bounded capability for a defined purpose under constraints.

**Capability** — an ability exposed by a tool, skill, agent, service, model or runtime.

**Conformance** — evidence that an implementation satisfies normative requirements of a specification/profile.

**Control-plane tax** — additional latency/cost/resources caused by orchestration, policy, state, verification, telemetry and evidence rather than direct task work.

**Evidence** — artifact or observation supporting a specific claim.

**False Completion** — system declares success while the required real-world outcome is not satisfied.

**Human Effort per Verified Outcome (HEVO)** — human attention/time required per verified successful outcome.

**Intelligence System Contract** — working name for a vendor-neutral machine-readable contract composing mission, state, authority, resources, assurance and adjacent protocols.

**ISE** — Intelligence Systems Engineering, candidate systems-level discipline/abstraction.

**Mission** — bounded intended outcome with constraints, authority, resources and acceptance criteria.

**MISSION-Bench** — proposed benchmark family for long-horizon verified outcomes.

**Principal** — entity on whose behalf authority is held or exercised: human, agent, service, workload, organization or device.

**Trajectory** — ordered record of observations, decisions, actions and state transitions during execution.

**VAIE** — Verified Adaptive Intelligence Engineering, candidate methodology.

**Verified Outcome** — outcome whose required acceptance criteria are satisfied by qualifying evidence.

**VSR** — Verified Success Rate.

**WVOU** — Weekly Verified Outcomes per Active User.
