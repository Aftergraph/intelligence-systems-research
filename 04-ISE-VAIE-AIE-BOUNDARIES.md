# ISE, VAIE and AIE Boundaries

## Intelligence Systems Engineering (ISE)
Candidate field/system abstraction.

Question:
How are complete intelligent systems engineered across models, software, compute, agents, tools, people and environments?

Potential concerns:
perception, state, reasoning, memory, execution, assurance, evidence, HCI, runtime, economics, security.

## Verified Adaptive Intelligence Engineering (VAIE)
Candidate methodology inside ISE.

Working lifecycle:
Intent → Mission → State/Capabilities/Authority → Execution → Observation → Assurance → Evidence → Verified Outcome → Recovery/Adaptation.

VAIE is not yet established as novel.

## Agentic Institution Engineering (AIE)
Separate research program.

Question:
How is legitimate authority established, delegated, constrained, budgeted, revoked and evidenced across agent systems?

AIE may later serve as an institutional-authority layer in broader ISE systems, but:
- AIE evidence does not automatically validate ISE,
- ISE must not absorb AIE merely for architectural neatness,
- each track retains separate claims, experiments and publication lineage.
