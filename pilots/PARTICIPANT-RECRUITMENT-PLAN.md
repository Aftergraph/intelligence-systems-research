## 2. Screening Criteria

### Must-Have (Eligibility)
| Criteria | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **AI tool usage** | Must use AI tools (chat-based LLMs, assistants, or AI features in IDE/tools) at least weekly |
| **Language** | Fluent in English (minimum for sessions); Danish speakers eligible for Danish-language session variants |
| **Age** | 18+ years |
| **Timezone** | CEST/CET (±2 hours) for synchronous sessions, or flexible scheduling for async tasks |

### Preferred (Recruitment Priority)
| Criteria | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Role** | Software engineers, product managers, data analysts, or operators working with AI tools in production |
| **Experience** | 1+ years using AI tools for work tasks |
| **Session type** | Can attend ~60–90 min session during business hours (09:00–18:00 CEST) |

### Exclusion Criteria
| Criteria | Reason |
|----------|--------|
| Professional UX researchers or HCI researchers | May introduce demand characteristics |
| Participants in competing HCI studies (last 30 days) | To avoid priming/confounds |
| Cannot complete all 4 tasks within session | Protocol requires full task battery |
