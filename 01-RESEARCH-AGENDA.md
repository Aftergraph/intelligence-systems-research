# Research Agenda

## Umbrella question
How should reliable adaptive intelligent systems be engineered beyond the model level?

## Primary research question
**RQ0:** Are existing engineering disciplines and standards sufficient for designing, operating, evaluating and governing long-horizon adaptive intelligent systems?

### Allowed conclusions
- **D1 — REJECT:** No meaningful systems-level gap.
- **D2 — INTEGRATE:** Useful interdisciplinary framework, but no new discipline justified.
- **D3 — INVESTIGATE:** Distinct systems-level gap worthy of formal and empirical work.

Only D3 justifies a serious scientific case for ISE.
